# Content Framework

## Core Project Structure

### 背景与目标

State the user or business problem, the limitation of the previous state, and the intended outcome. Keep it to one compact paragraph or bullet. Avoid opening with implementation details.

Useful pattern:

```text
原有场景/链路存在什么问题；目标是建立什么能力，并改善什么结果。
```

### 产品与链路

Describe the product decision and end-to-end operating chain. Use arrows when they improve comprehension. Include technology only when it explains a product tradeoff, capability boundary, or delivery mechanism.

Useful pattern:

```text
输入/触发 -> 判断或路由 -> 核心处理 -> 结果呈现 -> 反馈闭环
```

### 数据与指标

Prefer comparable evidence:

- baseline to result;
- time or steps reduced;
- accuracy, success rate, conversion, adoption, cost, or throughput;
- shipped milestone when quantitative data is unavailable.

Do not imply causality that the source cannot support. Distinguish offline evaluation, online behavior, and business outcomes.

### 我的职责

Name the decisions and boundaries personally owned: product definition, interaction, evaluation system, model selection, standards, stakeholder coordination, rollout, or iteration. Avoid repeating the entire team's output.

## Compression Rules

- Give full four-module treatment to the two to four projects most relevant to the target role.
- Merge repetitive methods and remove generic task narration.
- Keep one claim per sentence where possible.
- Replace vague verbs with decision-bearing verbs: 主导、定义、设计、建立、推动、验证、迭代.
- Do not repeat the same metric in positioning, project body, and result box.

## Credibility Audit

Flag for confirmation when:

- the same metric has conflicting values;
- a result is unusually high without sample size or measurement definition;
- the date range overlaps implausibly or lacks explanation;
- a company-wide result is attributed to one person without ownership detail;
- a confidential commercial figure may not be publishable;
- a personal project has no verifiable anchor despite strong claims.

Keep flagged items in a separate audit list. Do not silently invent a cleaner number.

## Positioning Test

The top section passes when a recruiter can answer these questions in five seconds:

1. What role is this candidate targeting?
2. What product domain do they specialize in?
3. What is the strongest evidence of fit?
4. How can they be contacted or verified?
